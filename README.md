## About the Library

Al-Masdar Islamic Library is established to serve the interests of the Ummah and to support the study and practice of sound Islamic knowledge in society.

The library aims to make selected Islamic books easier to discover and read through an organised digital service, while also developing a physical library.

Our book collection is selected with particular attention to the soundness of Islamic belief (`aqeedah`) and the reliability of the material.

## Library Services

### Online PDF Library

Registered users can:

- Search for books by title and explore the available collection.
- Browse books by category.
- View book information.
- Read available PDF books through the website's PDF Reader.
- Access their account and view their PDF reading history.

**An account login is required to read PDF books online.** The Telegram bot can help users find books and open the relevant website reader page; it does not send PDF files directly.

### Physical Book Borrowing

The library also facilitates physical book borrowing. Users can submit a borrow request through the website or visit the library in person.

Borrowing requests and book assignments are managed through the library's administration system. Where a user already has an active borrowed book, additional requests may remain pending until an administrator approves them.

The physical library is currently under development. Its services should not be considered fully open to the public until an official announcement is made.

### Notifications

The library uses SMS notifications for borrowing-related updates and other relevant library information.

## How to Access

1. Visit the [Al-Masdar Islamic Library website](https://al-masdar.free.nf).
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Search or browse the available books.
4. Open a book's reader page and sign in to read its PDF, where available.

You can also use the [Al-Masdar Library Telegram Bot](https://t.me/AlMasdarLibraryBot) to search for books, view book details and access the website's PDF Reader.

## Book Selection and Copyright

The library prioritises books that meet its standards for sound Islamic belief and responsible knowledge-sharing.

Books obtained from online sources are included only when the author or publisher has granted permission, or when the material is clearly stated to be freely distributed. See [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) for the collection and distribution principles.
