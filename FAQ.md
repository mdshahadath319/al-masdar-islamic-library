# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I use Al-Masdar Islamic Library?

Visit the [official website](https://al-masdar.free.nf), create an account or sign in, and browse the available collection.

## Do I need an account to read PDF books?

Yes. You must be signed in to the website to read PDF books through the online PDF Reader.

## Can I search for books?

Yes. You can search the collection and browse books by category on the website.

## What can the Telegram bot do?

The [Al-Masdar Library Telegram Bot](https://t.me/AlMasdarLibraryBot) can search for books, show book details and provide access to the relevant website PDF Reader page.

## Does the Telegram bot send PDF files?

No. The bot provides a link to the website's PDF Reader. You must sign in to the website to read the book.

## Can I borrow a physical book?

The library supports physical book borrowing requests. You can submit a request through the website or visit the library in person. Requests and assignments are managed through the library's administration system.

The physical library is currently under development. Check the official website for announcements about its public availability.

## What happens if I already have a book borrowed?

Additional borrowing requests may remain pending while you have an active borrowed book. An administrator can review and approve further borrowing requests.

## How long can I keep a borrowed book?

The borrowing period is defined by the library. Please check the information provided by the library when your request is approved. This repository does not specify a fixed borrowing duration.

## Does the library send notifications?

Yes. SMS notifications are used for borrowing-related updates and other relevant library information.

## Who develops and maintains the website?

The website is developed and maintained by **1Sec Technologies Limited**.

## Where can I get help?

You can visit the [1Sec Technologies Limited Help Center](https://1sectools.alwaysdata.net/). It is a general Help Center for 1Sec Technologies Limited products, not a dedicated Al-Masdar support portal.
