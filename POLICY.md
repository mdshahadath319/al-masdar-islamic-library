# Collection and Distribution Policy

## Purpose

Al-Masdar Islamic Library aims to serve the Ummah by making selected Islamic books and learning resources easier to discover and access.

The library seeks to promote sound Islamic knowledge and responsible reading.

## Book Selection

The library gives priority to books that meet its standards for sound and accurate Islamic belief (`aqeedah`).

Books are considered for inclusion with attention to the reliability and suitability of their content. Inclusion in the collection should not be interpreted as a claim that every possible scholarly disagreement has been resolved.

## Sources of Books

Books may be obtained through:

- Physical books scanned for the library's collection.
- Digital materials obtained from online sources.

The source of a file does not, by itself, establish permission to reproduce or distribute it.

## Copyright and Permission

A PDF obtained from an online source may be included only when at least one of the following conditions is met:

1. The author or publisher has granted permission for its inclusion or distribution; or
2. The book or file is clearly stated by an authorised source to be freely distributed.

The library should not treat a file as freely distributable merely because it is publicly accessible on the internet.

Where permission or distribution status cannot be established, the file should not be included as a distributable library resource until the necessary rights or confirmation are obtained.

## Responsible Access

The library provides online PDF reading through its website. Users must sign in to access the PDF Reader.

Access controls on the website do not replace copyright permission. The library's responsibility to respect authors' and publishers' rights applies regardless of the technical method used to provide access.

## Physical Library

The physical library is under development. Any future public services will be communicated through the library's official channels.

## Policy Updates

This document describes the library's currently confirmed collection and distribution principles. It may be updated if the library formally adopts additional policies.
